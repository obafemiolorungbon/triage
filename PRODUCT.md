# Product Context: Triage

## Register

product

## Product Purpose

Triage is a self-hosted feedback platform for product, support, and engineering teams. It gives companies a configurable web widget, AI triage, escalation rules, internal handling, and optional handoff to tools like Linear and Jira.

## Primary Users

- Product teams reviewing patterns in customer feedback
- Support reps routing and resolving incoming requests
- Engineering teams receiving structured issues from real users
- Self-hosting companies that care about control, data ownership, and transparent deployment

## Tone

Calm, technical, trustworthy, and operational. The interface should feel like a reliable workbench, not a marketing brochure. Copy should be concrete and short.

## Design Intent

The internal platform should prioritize scan speed, clear status, compact controls, and consistent actions. The widget should feel polished and compact enough to sit inside another company product without stealing attention. The playground can be more expressive because it demonstrates the product in context.

## Anti-References

- Generic purple SaaS dashboards
- Neon glow effects
- Beige startup landing pages
- Over-carded admin layouts
- Empty hero claims without product surface
- Decorative UI that reduces scan speed
